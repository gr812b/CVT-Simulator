# Record to preserve when selecting the manuscript case set

Keep one campaign's `campaign.json`, `competitors_resolved.csv`, common course JSON/profile and exact selected `resolved_case.json` files. That records the physical knobs, unchanged vehicle boundaries, actuator identities, mass-moment edits, initial state and numerical controls.

For each figure or claim, name the competitor IDs, course sector or event IDs, observation window, and whether it is a full-course performance comparison or a local mechanism diagnostic. Preserve censored/incomplete competitors when their limitation is discussed.

Keep the per-car event records and sector first-passage times. They separate inherited lead from sector performance and identify exact support/contact transitions. Retain raw native solver states for a final numerical check.

Record runtime with environment, worker count, setup/integration/post-processing timing and solver settings. Parallel wall times are not controlled single-core benchmarks.

Before manuscript promotion: resolve any review flags; compare selected cases with tighter controls, finer diagnostic sampling and a changed checkpoint interval; verify the same physical explanation survives. No table of discovery-search attempts is needed in the paper. Keep the selected physical cases and the compact scope of the exploration instead.
