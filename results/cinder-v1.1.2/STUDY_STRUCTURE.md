# CINDER v1.1.2 study structure

This document defines repository organization only. It does not select manuscript figures or promote any study output to an official paper result.

## Root contract

Every maintained study keeps the following at study root:

- `README.md`: scientific question, run instructions, interpretation boundary, and local context;
- `study.json`: standardized common envelope plus study-specific content;
- `run.py`: canonical maintained-study entry point;
- `verify_study.py`: local verification entry point where one exists;
- `FORMULATION_LINKAGE.md`: optional derivation-to-study map;
- interpretation/recap PDFs: retained at root until the corresponding Results section is finalized.

## Standard directories

Directories are created only when a study needs them.

- `infrastructure/`: support libraries, case helpers, mathematical helpers, or reusable study plumbing. Not run directly as the study.
- `experiments/`: maintained protocol/case-family implementations.
- `analysis/`: maintained post-processing, synthesis, metrics, and candidate figure/table generation. This is not a paper-official designation.
- `exploration/`: discovery-only scripts/notes intentionally excluded from `run.py`.
- `provenance/`: source registration, reconstruction notes, external-data records, and related evidence.
- `tests/`: study-local automated tests.
- `artifacts/`: generated outputs. This directory is ignored by Git.

## Canonical run rule

`python <study>/run.py` is the single maintained reproduction entry point. It must be capable of regenerating every maintained candidate output that could later support the manuscript. Once Chapter 4 figure selection is frozen, this becomes the stronger requirement that every cited figure/table from the study is regenerated by that command.

`exploration/` is deliberately outside this rule.

The common `paper_role` remains `undecided` during cleanup. Existing study-local fields that use labels such as `official` are preserved for backward compatibility and mean only that the experiment belongs to that maintained study; they do not imply manuscript selection.

## Manifest rule

All studies use the common schema envelope in `infrastructure/study.schema.json`. Scientific details remain free-form under `experiments` and `study_specific`, so standardization does not force unlike studies into identical experimental designs.
