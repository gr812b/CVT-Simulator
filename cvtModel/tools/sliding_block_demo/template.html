Scope / assumptions: These are deliberately synthetic profile examples, not scanned CVTech production geometry. Each block translates in one radial–axial plane, keeps its orientation, and has identical motion. Contact pressure, friction, 3-D saddle geometry, finite guide strength and transitions between different contact branches are not resolved. *The guide moment is the ideal reaction required to prevent rocking; it is displayed but not compared with a measured guide capacity. A contact shown at a face endpoint is treated as a rounded point/nose approximation; use a measured rounded profile for production mechanics. Inertia parameters represent the TOTAL block mass.
