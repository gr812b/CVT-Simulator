What this section establishes

Filled, hatched regions represent material. The two bold contact faces are part of those solids. Only the moving assembly translates; fixed outlines stay unchanged throughout travel.

The finite inserts cover the swept contact locations with a 1 mm radial margin, within the original profile domains. They end before the unused continuations cross. This is a reference envelope around the selected branch, not measured CVTech hardware.

The belt is 10 mm deep and 30 mm wide at its pitch line, in a 20° half-angle groove. Closing the groove moves it outward by Δr = x / (2 tan 20°). These dimensions provide context and do not enter the weight mechanics.

Failure cases: the short-track sample exhausts the solver's full-footprint domain. The parallel sample cannot select a unique weight pose. Neither displays an unsuccessful solver iterate as a physical state.

Synthetic geometry; frictionless, fixed-orientation weight motion. This is an operating-point probe, not a transient simulation. *The guide moment is the required reaction, not a guide-strength check. Contact pressure, friction, recontact, 3-D shaping and spring forces are not modeled. No measured CVTech geometry is claimed.
